# శ్రీ కనకధారా స్తోత్రం · Śrī Kanakadhārā Stōtram

## మహాత్మ్యం · Māhātmya

బాలసన్యాసిగా భిక్షార్థం సంచరిస్తున్న ఆదిశంకరులు ఒక నిరుపేద బ్రాహ్మణ గృహిణి ఇంటికి చేరగా, ఆమె వద్ద ఇవ్వడానికి ఏమీ లేక ఒక్క ఎండిన ఆమలకఫలం మాత్రమే ఉండేది; అయినా అపారభక్తితో దానినే భిక్షగా సమర్పించింది. Moved by her pure generosity amid poverty, young Ādi Śaṅkara composed this hymn to Mother Lakṣmī; pleased by the prayer, Lakṣmī is said to have showered golden āmalaka fruits upon the home — hence *Kanakadhārā*, “the stream or shower of gold.”

Lakṣmī beholds Mukunda, the source of bliss, with eyes half-closed in love and stillness. May those delicate eyes of the consort of the serpent-couch Lord become the cause of my welfare.

Her series of glances shines on the dark chest of Viṣṇu, near the Kaustubha gem, like a sapphire garland. Such glances fulfill even the Lord's desires; may they bring me good fortune.

By the power of her glance, even Manmatha first gained a place in the auspicious Lord Madhusūdana. May that slow, gentle half-glance of the ocean's daughter fall upon me.

<Meaning>
Even those of limited merit can gain heaven through her moisture-laden compassionate look. May the lotus-bright glance of the lotus-seated Goddess cultivate nourishment and fullness in me.
</Meaning>
