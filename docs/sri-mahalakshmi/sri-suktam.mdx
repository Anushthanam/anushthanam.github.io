<Meaning>
The Sri Suktam invokes Goddess Lakshmi — golden-hued, radiant, seated on the lotus — praying for her presence, prosperity, cattle, gold and progeny, and for the banishing of alakshmi (misfortune, poverty and inauspiciousness) from one's home.
</Meaning>
